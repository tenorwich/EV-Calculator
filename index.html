Method. Charging cost is the car’s energy (miles ÷ mi/kWh) multiplied by the rate in force: the flat import rate, or in Two-rate mode the off-peak rate for night charging and the peak rate for day charging. Fuel cost is miles ÷ mpg × 4.54609 litres per UK gallon × the price per litre. If your mi/kWh is measured at the battery rather than at the wall, real charging draws roughly 10% more.
